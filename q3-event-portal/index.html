Registration Instructions

  1. Fill in the registration form below with your name, register number and chosen event.
  2. Note your registration ID shown on the screen – you need it to check status.
  3. Team events: only the team leader registers, listing all members at the help desk.
  4. Registration closes 3 days before each event or when the participant limit is reached.
  5. Carry your college ID card on the day of the event.
